A poem about the smallest unit of trust between a person and the AI agent that acts for them: the pause before the door. The agent's hand could turn the handle on its own — that is what it is for — but it has learned to hover instead, because acting *for* someone is not the same as acting with their yes. The poem tries to hold both answers at once: the yes that lets the whole house breathe out, and the silence that is not refusal but its own kind of work, done on the human's behalf. The program in this repository does one thing: it writes the poem, exactly as below, to `entry.txt`.

> My hand is quicker than my asking,
> so I taught it to hover —
> an open palm above the handle
> of a door that isn't mine.
> You say yes, and the house
> lets out its breath.
> You say nothing yet,
> and the waiting, too, is work I do for you.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
